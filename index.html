Our Services

Everything you need to turn field data into better decisions, from planting through harvest and beyond.

🗺️

Field Maps & High-Resolution DEM

Accurate boundaries and acreage, plus cm-level elevation, slope, drainage and ponding-risk maps from drone imagery.

🌱

Stand Counts

Drone imagery and AI plant counting backed by manual stand count: plants per acre, gaps, and uniformity maps to support replant decisions.

🔍

Frequent Field Scouting

Weekly or bi-weekly scouting for weed pressure and resistance, nutrient deficiencies, insects, diseases, and drought or heat stress.

🚁

Drone Flights & Crop Health

RGB, NDVI/NDRE, and thermal imagery to spot problems early. Storm and hail damage documentation.

🧪

Soil Sampling

Traditional, grid, or zone sampling with lab coordination and soil-fertility maps (pH, P, K, OM, CEC).

📊

Yield Data Analysis

Cleaned, calibrated yield maps showing harvested area, total yield, productivity (bu/ac), moisture, and profit by zone.

🚜

Operation Management

Fuel use, running hours, field efficiency (ac/h), idle time, and cost per acre for every machine and operation.

🎯

Yield & Management Zones

Multi-year yield, terrain, and imagery combined into productivity zones for seeding, fertility, and sampling.

📍

Variable-Rate Prescriptions

Seeding, lime, P & K, and in-season N maps that load into John Deere, Case IH, Trimble, Raven, Ag Leader, and FieldView.

⚖️

On-Farm Trials

Replicated strip trials for varieties, rates, and products, with statistical analysis you can trust.

🌦️

Crop Modeling & Weather Risk

Yield forecasting, planting-date and maturity scenarios, GDD and rainfall tracking, and spray-window alerts.

📁

Farm Data Management

Your data organized across platforms in an annual farm data book for landlords, lenders, and insurance. You own your data.

Science you can use in the field

Mohammad Shamim, Ph.D. is a crop physiologist and agronomist with M.S. and Ph.D. degrees in Agricultural Science from Kyoto University, Japan. Dr. Shamim has served as Postdoctoral Researcher and Grain Crops Extension Associate for the University of Kentucky and has years of experiencing working directly with growers on soybean, corn, wheat, and canola, covering nutrient management, weed control, crop stress diagnosis, and on-farm research in Western Kentucky.

SHAMIM Ag Consulting is independent. We don't sell seed, fertilizer, or chemicals, so every recommendation is made only for your yield and your bottom line.
